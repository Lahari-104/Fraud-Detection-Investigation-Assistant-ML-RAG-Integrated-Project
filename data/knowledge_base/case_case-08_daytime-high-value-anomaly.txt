# title: Daytime high-value anomaly
# grounded_features: amount, hour_of_day, anomaly_components, probability

A $260 transaction at 14:30 was flagged despite unremarkable timing, driven almost entirely by an extreme V17 anomaly contribution. It was confirmed fraudulent, illustrating that a strong anomaly profile can carry a flag without an overnight timestamp.
