# type: case
# id: CASE-07
# title: Small-amount flag wrongly dismissed
# grounded_features: amount, hour_of_day, anomaly_components, probability

A $0.95 flagged transaction was auto-approved under the old under-$5 fast-approve rule. It was later confirmed as the opening probe of a fraud run that escalated to a $600 loss. This case drove the withdrawal of the small-amount approval exception.
