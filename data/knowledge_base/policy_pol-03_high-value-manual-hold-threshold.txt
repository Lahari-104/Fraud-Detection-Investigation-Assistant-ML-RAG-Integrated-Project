# type: policy
# id: POL-03
# title: High-Value Manual-Hold Threshold
# grounded_features: amount, hour_of_day, anomaly_components, probability

Any flagged transaction with an amount at or above $1,000 requires manual analyst sign-off before release. This is the fraud 99th-percentile band ($1,357) and false negatives here are the most costly. Automated approval is not permitted for flagged transactions above this threshold regardless of model probability.
