# title: High-value manual-hold catch
# grounded_features: amount, hour_of_day, anomaly_components, probability

A $1,420 transaction was flagged just above the fraud 99th-percentile band. Model probability was a moderate 0.64, which alone might have been released, but the high-value manual-hold policy forced analyst sign-off. Manual review confirmed fraud and prevented the largest single loss in that day's batch.
