# type: policy
# id: POL-11
# title: High-Amount Daytime Anomaly Handling
# grounded_features: amount, hour_of_day, anomaly_components, probability

A high-value transaction (>= $200) flagged during ordinary daytime hours with a strong anomaly profile should not be downgraded merely because its timing is unremarkable. Roughly 17% of fraud exceeds $200, so amount and anomaly profile can carry a flag on their own without an overnight timestamp.
