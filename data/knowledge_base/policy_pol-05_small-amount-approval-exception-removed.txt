# type: policy
# id: POL-05
# title: Small-Amount Approval Exception Removed
# grounded_features: amount, hour_of_day, anomaly_components, probability

The legacy rule that auto-approved transactions under $5.00 to reduce friction is withdrawn. Card-testing exploits exactly that exception; small amount alone must never override a model flag. Supersedes any prior low-value fast-approve guidance.
